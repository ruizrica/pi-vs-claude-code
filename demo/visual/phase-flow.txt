PHASE MODES
═══════════

UNDERSTAND:  Interactive (user conversation)
GATHER:      Parallel   (multiple scouts simultaneously)
PLAN:        Sequential (one planner after gather)
EXECUTE:     Parallel   (multiple builders simultaneously)
REVIEW:      Sequential (reviewer after execute, loops 3x max)
